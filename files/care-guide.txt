NOVASHOP - PRODUCT CARE GUIDE
=============================

Thank you for shopping with NovaShop. This short guide helps you keep your
purchases in great shape.

Electronics
- Keep devices dry and away from direct heat.
- Clean charging contacts with a dry, soft cloth.
- Store batteries at 50% charge if unused for a long time.

Home & Kitchen
- Ceramic items: avoid sudden temperature changes.
- Stainless flasks: rinse with warm water after each use.

Fashion
- Wash cotton items inside-out at 30C.
- Denim: wash sparingly to preserve the fade.

If you have questions about a specific product, use the Contact page and our
team will help you out.
