NOVASHOP - WARRANTY POLICY
==========================

1. Electronics carry a 1-year limited warranty from the delivery date.
2. Home and Sports products carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing
   defects.
3. Fashion items can be returned unworn within 30 days.

How to claim:
- Open a ticket from the Contact page with your order number and a short
  description of the issue.
- Our team replies within one business day with return instructions.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, moisture ingress, or normal
wear and tear.
